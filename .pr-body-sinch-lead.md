## Summary

Adds an Inquiry column and source pills on the CRM Leads table, and a right-side lead detail drawer for Sinch chat fields (contact, inquiry, page URL, referrer, conversation id). Convert to VIP reuses the existing handler. Sinch inbox deep links are deliberately not included: Sinch does not expose a stable conversation URL pattern.

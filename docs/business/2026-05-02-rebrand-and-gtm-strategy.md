# Rebrand + GTM strategy (2026-05-02 brainstorm)

Strategic decisions from the 2026-05-02 brainstorm session. Captured here so they survive past chat history.

**Updated 2026-05-03:** consumer-facing DBA renamed from Tire Syndicate to Tire Triad (`tiretriad.com` acquired via Cloudflare). "Triad" reads as alliance / trio (Alex+DJ+Kyle / Michelin+BFG+Uniroyal / drives+steers+trailer), avoids the organized-crime undertones of "syndicate", and pairs cleaner with the formal Front Range Rubber LLC.

## Trigger

- Partnership exit from Travis / Skedaddle FedEx forces rebrand of the tire-resale side.
- Concurrent reality check: 1,629 clicks / $3-loss sale on a recent KO3 listing surfaced that consumer FB Marketplace in NOCO is dominated by fraud-fueled lowball pricing. Skedaddle's honest landed cost can't compete on price alone.

## Structural facts (favorable)

- eFleet account is preserved, in Alex's name.
- Delivery infrastructure preserved (Alex + DJ).
- Domain `frontrangerubber.com` is owned by Alex.
- No legal time pressure on the rebrand; tire side cleanly separates from Travis's Skedaddle FedEx.

## Decisions

### Brand structure

**Dual-brand, single operation:**

- **Front Range Rubber LLC** — legal entity, commercial-fleet-facing. W9-issuing. Phone, email, web presence under this name. Boring-on-purpose vendor energy. Commercial outreach uses this brand exclusively.
- **Tire Triad** (DBA) — consumer-facing FB Marketplace seller name. No domain (parked .com asks $2k; not justified for an unvalidated channel; revisit if TS earns its way in). Possible IG handle. Consumer FB listings sign under this name.
- Same operations, same eFleet account, same warehouse, same delivery, same Alex + DJ.

### Positioning constraint

**Never claim Michelin authorization, dealer status, or eFleet relationship in any external-facing material.**

- ❌ "Michelin authorized reseller" / "dealer" / "partner" / "direct"
- ✅ "We carry new Michelin and BFGoodrich commercial truck tires" (factual product, no relationship claim)
- Buyer-facing answer to "how do you have these prices": *"Wholesale sourcing. Long story. The savings come down to no retail overhead and we move inventory."*

### Pricing structure

Two prices for the same SKU, audience-segregated:

- **Tire Triad consumer price** — market-clearing on FB Marketplace. Lowball pond, lowball floor.
- **Front Range Rubber commercial price** — wholesale-plus-honest-margin. Quoted per-fleet via cold-call follow-up. Fleet buyer never sees the consumer price.

### GTM motion (primary)

Front Range Rubber commercial cold-call outreach. The smallest viable process:

1. Target list: 50 NOCO small-fleet operators (HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, towing, ag, pest control, mobile mechanics, fence, snow removal, tree service, septic, propane, oil & gas service, regional owner-operator trucking). 2-15 vehicles each. Skip 50+ truck operators (already on national programs).
2. Opener (no Michelin attribution):
   > "Hi [name], this is [your name] from Front Range Rubber in Loveland. We carry new Michelin and BFGoodrich commercial truck tires at prices that come in below Discount Tire shelf. Local delivery, fleet invoicing, no minimums. Mind if I send you a quote next time you're rotating?"
3. CRM all responses through the existing portal CRM module. Re-call "not now" buckets in 60 days.
4. First test: 25 calls. If 1+ fleet asks for a quote, signal. If 0, script or segment is wrong.

## Riskiest assumption

That NOCO has 50+ small fleets currently paying retail at Discount Tire who would switch for a better price + W9-able vendor + local delivery. Falsifiable in 25 cold calls.
